# Chrome and Safari beta test

Use **Safari on iPhone** or **Chrome on Android** as the primary phone test. Desktop Safari and Chrome are also supported. Use an up-to-date browser and open the supplied HTTPS release link directly in it. Test the published release containing the recovery fixes; local results do not change the GitHub Pages app until it is published.

Record the device model, OS version, browser version, and audio output (built-in speaker, wired headphones, or Bluetooth). Start with the app visible, volume above zero, and trainers disabled.

## Playback and controls

1. Open the app, press Play, and confirm both sound and the beat indicator start. Stop and restart several times.
2. Change between 4/4, 3/4, 6/8 and 7/8; try both 7/8 groupings. Meter changes stop playback intentionally. Press Play after each change and confirm it starts from beat 1 with sound.
3. Try all nine sounds. Test one bar of count-in, then confirm the selected click starts after the spoken count.
4. Change BPM and subdivisions while playing. Try the normal low and high tempo limits. Confirm controls remain responsive and there are no unexpected silences or bursts of old clicks.
5. Enable each trainer, then combine them. Confirm tempo/subdivision progression and deliberate silent gap bars. Try polyrhythm and switch back to the metronome.
6. Set a short bar-based stop timer. Confirm playback stops at the limit and Play starts a fresh session.
7. Open Training, Settings and Kit View during playback. Confirm playback continues, controls remain usable, and closing Kit View returns to the previous screen. Check sliders and the rhythm sheet on the phone's touch screen.
8. Stop and reload the page. Confirm preferences restore and Play works again.

## Stability and recovery

9. Keep the app visible and playing for ten minutes. Change a few controls during the run. Listen for unexplained stops, double clicks, or tempo changes.
10. Switch away and return, then lock/unlock the phone. If the browser interrupted playback, the app should show stopped and Play should recover it. Continuous screen-off/background playback is not a beta guarantee.
11. If relevant, disconnect/reconnect the headphones or Bluetooth device. Confirm Play works with the intended output afterward. Test recovery after a normal incoming call when convenient.

If playback freezes, leave the app visible for about two seconds, then tap Play after it returns to stopped. A detected frozen audio clock should recover without reopening the browser. Report any need to reload or close the browser.

## Report an issue

Include which step failed, active meter/BPM/sound/trainers, what you changed immediately beforehand, whether the beat indicator continued, and whether Stop/Start restored sound. Include the device/browser/output details above. A short screen recording with audio is useful when available.
