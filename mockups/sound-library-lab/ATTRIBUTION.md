# Audio sources and licences

## Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)

Source: https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL

Licence: Creative Commons Zero 1.0 Universal (CC0-1.0)

This lab uses edited excerpts of VCSL cowbell, hi-hat, cajón, and woodblock
recordings. The woodblock is used quietly beneath the spoken count. Files were
trimmed, converted to mono 48 kHz PCM, faded, and level-matched for metronome
auditioning.

## FreePats World Percussion

Source: https://github.com/freepats/world-percussion

Licence: Creative Commons Zero 1.0 Universal (CC0-1.0)

The Shaker and Tambourine palettes use edited real-instrument recordings from
this sound bank. Each role rotates through three alternate hits.

## Piper count-voice prototypes

Engine: https://github.com/rhasspy/piper

Models:

- John: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_US/john/medium
- Kristin: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_US/kristin/medium

Piper and the piper-voices repository use the MIT License. The John and
Kristin model cards identify their source recordings as public domain. The lab
contains generated excerpts for numbers one through ten plus offline
pitch-preserving tempo variants. The masters use spelled-out source text so
each complete word survives every tempo tier. Each full word is gently shifted
toward one shared pitch center per voice (approximately 95 Hz male and 137 Hz
female), preserving its natural internal inflection while reducing accidental
count-to-count accents. Bespoke production
recordings are still recommended if neither voice has the desired personality.
