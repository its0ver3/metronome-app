# Sound Shortlist

Standalone decision page for selecting the final metronome sound library. It
does not import or modify the production audio engine.

The user's nine checked choices were promoted into production. This page
remains available as the decision record and audition surface.

Open through the local Vite server at `/metronome-app/mockups/sound-shortlist/`.

## Included sounds

- The eight synthesized sounds that were available during the decision pass.
- VCSL cowbell, hi-hat, and cajón recordings from the sound-library lab.
- FreePats shaker and tambourine recordings from the sound-library lab.
- Male and female pitch-preserving count-voice prototypes, gently balanced
  around one pitch center per voice while preserving natural inflection.

Tap a sound pill for a consistent preview. The checkbox is independent of
playback, persists locally across refreshes, and starts with the already chosen
VCSL hi-hat and cajón checked.

The production result keeps Classic Click, Woodblock, Soft Tone, VCSL Cowbell,
VCSL Hi-hat, FreePats Shaker, FreePats Tambourine, Male Count, and Female Count.

Audio-source and licence details are recorded in
[`../sound-library-lab/ATTRIBUTION.md`](../sound-library-lab/ATTRIBUTION.md).
