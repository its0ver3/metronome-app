Sell a creator’s practice tool.

A recognizable identity, a few useful exercises, and a reliable daily metronome make a stronger offer than a recolored utility. The creator edition is the largest commercial improvement; the main screen and readable controls are the first design work to ship.

“Best” needs evidence beyond design.

Before making that claim, validate timing, background and locked-screen playback, interruptions, offline launch, and a complete purchase-to-install journey on supported phones. These mockups demonstrate a direction, not those capabilities or a revenue forecast.
