# Marketing Brief — Local Drum Shop Metronome

## Product

A simple mobile-first metronome app designed to help drummers practise. It includes optional gap, tempo, subdivision, accent, and polyrhythm controls, but it is still fundamentally a metronome.

## Business goal

Sell branded versions of the app to local drum shops. Each shop gets its own name, logo, colors, app icons, web address, and installable version while the underlying product remains shared and maintained.

## Customer and user

- **Customer:** local drum shop owners
- **End user:** the shop's drummer customers

## Value proposition

Give your customers a useful metronome app with your drum shop's brand on it—without having to design, build, or maintain an app yourself.

## Core message

**Your drum shop's own metronome app, ready for your customers.**

## Why a shop would want it

- Offers customers a practical branded tool they can use regularly.
- Keeps the shop present in customers' practice routines.
- Gives the shop its own app without the cost and complexity of building one.
- Is built for drum practice rather than presented as a generic music utility.

## Product plan

1. Finish and validate the core metronome.
2. Make shop branding, icons, metadata, hosting, and deployment repeatable.
3. Create a simple sales demo and branded mockup for prospective shops.
4. Pilot the offer with one or two local drum shops.
5. Use those pilots to refine pricing, onboarding, support, and rollout.

## Marketing direction

Lead with shop ownership and simplicity. Show the shop what its version could look like, then demonstrate the metronome. Training features are supporting value, not the main story.

## Guardrails

- Do not describe it as a practice platform, community, analytics product, or AI tool.
- Do not overstate timing accuracy or improved practice outcomes without testing.
- Do not promise native App Store distribution; the current product is an installable web app.
- Do not call the branding process turnkey until shop-specific builds and deployments are automated.
